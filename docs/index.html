Clip Automation

A personal tool that turns live-stream recordings into short-form clips.

What it does

This is a private, single-operator automation. It watches the operator's own live-streaming channels for newly published recordings, identifies the most engaging moments using audio analysis and an AI model, cuts those moments into short vertical clips, and adds captions.

Finished clips are sent to the operator for review. Nothing is published anywhere until the operator manually approves each individual clip. On approval, the clip is uploaded to the operator's own connected social accounts.

Who it is for

It is not a public product and has no end users other than its operator. There is no sign-up, and no third party can connect an account to it.

Platform connections

  • TikTok — uploads approved clips to the operator's own account as drafts.
  • YouTube — uploads approved clips to the operator's own channel.
