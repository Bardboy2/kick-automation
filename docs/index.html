How it works

Six stages, start to finish, with you involved exactly once.

1

Watches your channel

Polls Kick and Twitch every five minutes. The moment a new VOD appears, it starts — no prompting, no uploading.

2

Finds the peaks

Scans the full audio track for energy spikes — laughter, reactions, crowd noise, drops — to shortlist candidate moments.

3

Scores them with AI

Pulls a frame from each candidate and asks Claude how viral the moment actually is. Loud isn't the same as good.

4

Cuts and captions

Trims the top-ranked moments, burns in word-level captions timed to the speech, and adds your channel watermark.

5

Sends them to you

Finished clips land in Telegram with title, description and score. Approve, reject or rewrite the caption from your phone.

6

Posts automatically

One tap pushes to YouTube Shorts and TikTok with metadata already written. Nothing publishes without your say-so.

Volume isn't virality

Most auto-clippers just hunt for the loudest thirty seconds. That finds you a lot of shouting and not much worth posting.

This one extracts a frame from every candidate moment and has Claude judge it — what's happening, and would anyone stop scrolling for it. That judgement is blended with the audio signal, so a quiet moment with a great reaction can outrank a loud one with nothing in it.

  • Every moment scored 0–10 for viral potential
  • AI weighted 60%, audio energy 40%
  • Clips ranked, so you see the best one first
  • Tuned for IRL, Just Chatting and music streams
