Privacy Policy

Last updated: 17 October 2026

Clip Automation ("the Service") is a private, single-operator tool that creates short-form clips from the operator's own live-stream recordings and uploads them to the operator's own social media accounts. It has no public users and no sign-up process.

Information the Service handles

  • Access tokens. OAuth access and refresh tokens issued by TikTok and YouTube when the operator connects their own accounts. These authorise the Service to upload videos on the operator's behalf.
  • Video recordings. Publicly available recordings from the operator's own live-streaming channels, downloaded for processing.
  • Generated clips and metadata. Clip files, transcripts, captions, titles, and descriptions produced from those recordings.

Information the Service does not collect

The Service collects no personal information from any third party. It has no visitors, no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, no cookies, and no tracking of any kind. It does not read any other person's TikTok or YouTube data.

How information is used

Solely to produce clips and, after the operator manually approves each one, to upload that clip to the operator's own connected accounts. Access tokens are used only to perform those uploads. Information is never sold, rented, or shared, and is not used for advertising or profiling.

Storage and retention

Data is stored on a private server controlled solely by the operator. Access tokens are kept in a configuration file that is not published and is excluded from the project's public source repository. Downloaded recordings and generated clips are deleted automatically 48 hours after creation.

Third-party processing

Clip audio and still frames are sent to Anthropic's Claude API to identify engaging moments and generate captions. Approved clips are transmitted to TikTok and YouTube through their official APIs for upload. No other third party receives any data.

Security

The server is access-controlled and reachable only via SSH key authentication. Credentials are never committed to source control.

Revoking access

The operator may revoke the Service's access at any time from their TikTok account settings or their Google account permissions page. Revocation immediately and permanently ends the Service's ability to upload.

Children

The Service is not directed at children and has no users other than its operator.

Changes

Any update to this policy will be published on this page with a revised date above.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be raised through the project's public repository at github.com/Bardboy2/kick-automation.
